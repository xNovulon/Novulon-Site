Novulon's ZOI Overhaul

Everything in one window: zois that act more like people, an attraction system, cheats for anyone, and smoother play. Press the purple N next to your icons to open it. Every part has its own switch.

Download ZOI Overhaul FreeinZOI 0.10In-game mod
Install
  1. Remove the old onesHad StatCheats, AI Remade or Lag Remover? Delete their folders from Documents\inZOI\Mods\InGame first
  2. UnzipInto Documents\inZOI\Mods. It lands in the InGame folder there
  3. Load a saveStart inZOI and load a save like you always do
  4. Press NThe purple N next to your icons in the bottom right

Optional: with the game closed, open the mod's Start-up fixes folder and run Apply start-up fixes once. Undo start-up fixes puts everything back.
