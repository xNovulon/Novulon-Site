Tools for The Sims 4 on PC

Less waiting. More playing.

Free Windows apps for people who play The Sims 4 with a lot of mods. One gets the game loading faster and keeps your CC tidy. The other lets you make WickedWhims animations without Blender.

Sims Hub

The Sims 4 reads every CC file at start-up, even the ones nobody wears. Quick Start works out what your saves really use and starts the game with just that. Full Start brings everything back when you want to build or browse Create a Sim.

Download Sims Hub View on GitHub
Quick StartSame sims, same lots, a fraction of the loading time.
CC browserEvery file with its picture, sorted into hair, tops, furniture, animations and more.
Duplicates and mergesFinds copies hidden inside merged files, and splits merges back apart.
Missing CCShows what a save is missing and finds it in your Downloads, even inside .zip files.
Less lagTurns down only the graphics settings that cost the most.
Safe by designNothing is deleted, and Undo last change puts things back.

Wicked Animator

18+

Load your own sims from the Tray with their looks and outfits, pose them, add movement, faces and sound, and send the result to your Mods folder. It needs the WickedWhims mod, and only adult sims can be used.
